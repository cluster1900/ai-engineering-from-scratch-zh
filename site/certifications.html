HOW THIS WORKS

Learn. Build. Decide under pressure.

01

Learn the mechanism

Study the concepts behind the blueprint instead of memorizing product vocabulary.

02

Build the system

Use labs and artifacts to make architecture, tooling, evaluation, and safety decisions concrete.

03

Practice the judgment

Use original scenarios, timed mocks, and domain feedback to find your weak decisions.
